Frequently asked questions

Does ClickCart sell products directly?

No. ClickCart shares product information and buying guides. Any retailer links will take readers to that retailer's website.

Are prices and stock guaranteed?

No. Prices, offers and availability can change. Always confirm current details on the retailer's website.

How should products be recommended?

Recommendations should be based on product research, relevant specifications, suitability and honest comparisons. Do not publish fake ratings or reviews.

Does this website collect visitor information?

This starter version has no account system, contact form, analytics or tracking cookies. Update the privacy information if you add any third-party tools.
